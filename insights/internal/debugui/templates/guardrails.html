Service guardrails

Guardrails are rules you declare about how a service may communicate (L7 micro-segmentation) - unlike anomaly baselines, which are learned. A broken guardrail is a policy violation, surfaced in the Findings feed. Pick a service to see what it actually talks to, check the callers/callees/egress you want to allow, add egress hosts free-form, and adopt them (default enforce alerts on violation; use suggest to observe only).

Add a guardrail

Pick a service to load its observed callers, callees, egress and transactions, then adopt them as an allowlist below.

Attribute correlation

Define a rule that links a value seen on one span to a value seen on another span in the same trace, and alerts when they don't line up as expected. Suggestions come from this transaction's observed edges.
