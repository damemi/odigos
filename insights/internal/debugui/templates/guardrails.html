Service guardrails

Guardrails are rules you declare about how a service may communicate (L7 micro-segmentation) — unlike anomaly baselines, which are learned. A broken guardrail is a policy violation, surfaced in the Findings feed. Pick a service, check any seen callers/callees (not only ones already observed talking to it), add egress hosts free-form, and adopt them (default enforce alerts on violation; use suggest to observe only).
