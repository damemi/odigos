Anomaly policies

A policy fires an anomaly when a trace's deviations add up to a risk score at or above its threshold. Most of the time you only set the threshold - signal weights have sensible defaults. There is a global default plus optional overrides for one service or one transaction; the most specific policy wins (transaction → service → global) and fully replaces the broader one, and a disabled override silences alerting for its scope. Lower the threshold to catch more, raise it to cut noise. Distinct from learning policies, which decide when a class stops learning and starts scoring. Saved edits apply live, no restart.
