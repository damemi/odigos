Risk model

The default risk model every anomaly policy builds on. Each signal adds its weight (points) to a 0–100 risk score; dangerous sub-cases add on top of a class's base novelty. Read-only reference — tune the weights per scope under Detection. A signal alone lands at low 20 · medium 35 · high 50 · critical 75.
