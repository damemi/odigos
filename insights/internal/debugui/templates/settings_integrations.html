Integrations

Deliver findings to external backends. Everything here goes through the same /api/v1/integrations paths the product uses; the form is rendered from GET /integrations/catalog, so a new backend needs no UI work. A finding reaches an integration only when it is routed there: pick integrations on a detection policy (anomalies) or a guardrail (violations); the global detection policy's list is the default route. One replica (the lease holder) polls every 15s and sends; retries back off up to 10 attempts, then park as dead until retried from the deliveries page.
